People ask what the greenhouse cost. Here's the honest bill of materials, rounded and in local terms, for a 3 × 4 m bamboo frame housing six NFT channels.

- Bamboo, treated and cut to length — the biggest line item, and worth buying pre-treated
- Galvanized bolts, washers, and a spanner that fits exactly one size, apparently on purpose
- 50% shade netting, enough to tension like a drum and redo the two panels I tore
- Food-grade PVC channels and end caps, plus net pots by the hundred
- A 15 W pump with a timer — the heart of the whole system, and hilariously the cheapest
- 500 L tank, shaded, plus a roll of food-safe hose

What I'd skip: the fancy pH pen I bought first (a mid-range one with replaceable probes outlives it), and the pre-bent fittings I bent myself anyway.

What I'd buy more of: bolts, hose clamps, and time spent squaring the base before raising the frame. The bill comes out smaller than most people's monthly coffee budget — the expensive parts were always the lessons.
