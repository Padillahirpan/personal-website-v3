Harvest #12 went out the door at 6:40, an hour before the sun cleared the neighbor's roof. 4.2 kg across twelve pots — the heaviest batch so far, and the first where every head made weight.

The difference this cycle was boring: steadier EC (thank you, experiment), a timer fix on the pump, and harvesting before the heat instead of after breakfast. Cut, crated, weighed, logged — twelve minutes end to end.

Next batch I'm testing whether a two-day dark spell before harvest crisps the leaves the way it does for other growers. If it works, it goes in the Selada Ops runbook; if not, it dies in a log line like the rest.
