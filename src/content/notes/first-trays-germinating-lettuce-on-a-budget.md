The whole germination setup cost less than a nice dinner: two nursery trays, a bag of rockwool cubes, one packet of butterhead seed, and a spray bottle that used to hold cat shampoo.

Week one was humbling. I overwatered the first tray and learned the word "damping off" the hard way — a fuzzy graveyard where seedlings used to be. The second tray stayed barely moist, and the germination rate went from miserable to 87%.

The routine that stuck: soak cubes, drop one seed per cube, spray morning and evening, and give them light but not sun. Two weeks later the strongest seedlings went into the first NFT channels, and the project stopped being an idea.
